WORKING TERRITORY
SEP 2026

A publishing project in which the digital layer should behave less like an effect and more like a second reading system. The interesting question is not “how can a page come alive?” but what information, state, viewpoint or meaning becomes possible only when the printed and computational versions are read together?

01 — hidden / absent states

The device decodes what print withholds.

The strongest digital layer is not extra decoration. It lets the reader access a state the physical object deliberately cannot hold on its own.

CASE 01

Voidopolis

Kat Mustatea / MIT Press — 2023

A book that can only be fully read through its computational layer. Images and language decay in the app over the course of a year, turning disappearance into part of the reading experience.

The technology does not illustrate loss. It performs it.

CASE 03

The Met Replica

The Met × Verizon × UNIT9 — 2023

Visitors scan real artworks, collect digital elements inspired by them, then carry those elements into Roblox as avatar items. The physical encounter produces something that has an afterlife elsewhere.

Encounter → extract → carry away.

CASE 04

CANAL Magazine

Sunday Afternoon / New York

A beautifully designed bilingual magazine about Canal Street. AR lifts and extends the oversized typography, while later issues use the layer to carry additional film and editorial material.

Print identity → motion identity.

The mechanic is expected. The execution is good enough that it does not matter.
